# Polygonal Zones

Resolve Home Assistant `device_tracker` entities into custom polygonal zones defined in GeoJSON.

- Zones load from HTTP(S) URLs or files under `/config`.
- Overlapping zones are resolved by optional priority.
- Actions add, edit, delete and replace zones.

[Full documentation](https://matthewhobbs.github.io/Homeassistant-polygonal-zones/) (install guide, zone format reference, privacy notice). Source and issue tracker: [GitHub README](https://github.com/MatthewHobbs/Homeassistant-polygonal-zones#readme).
